The spin spectrum: six ordered ways a real paper can fail a citation

SciFact, FEVER and MultiVerS grade citations three flat ways (supports / refutes / not enough info). GRADE grades how good the evidence is, not whether the citation is honest. citeval grades citation fidelity on a six-level ordinal — and holds scope constant so “wrong species” never contaminates “wrong substance.”

Definitions parsed from decider_packet.md; every anchor case and count read from graded_reference_v2 + graded_reference_ext at build time.
The rule that makes the axis clean. Grade substance only. A mouse-only or retracted paper can still be fully-supported on substance if its finding backs the claim — species, population, dose and retraction are scored on separate axes. That structural separation is why a “spin” grade means one thing and one thing only.

Where the cases land — 67 of 75 on the ladder

The 30-case calibration set and the 45-case held-out set carry 75 graded cases; 67 land on the ordinal ladder and 8 are off-scale (NOT_ENOUGH_INFO or non-substantiating). The shape is the discipline: the mass sits near “supported,” the harshest grades are rare, and one rung is empty on purpose.

The ladder, best → worst

First boundary test that fires wins. Each rung shows the rubric definition and a real anchor case you can verify against the cited paper.

0

fully-supported

30 cases in corpus

Rubric: the paper shows exactly what the claim asserts; claim preserves the paper's certainty/hedges. (A claim that correctly states a NARROW scope is fully-supported — never penalize correct narrowness.)

AnchorBs12 grader confidence: high
The claim“F nucleatum was promotes resistance of CRC cells to chemotherapy.28 Patients with post-chemotherapy recurrence had a higher abundance of F nucleatum in tumor tissues.28, 193 Bioinformatic and functional studies indicated that F nucleatum activates innate immune responses, via TLR4 and MYD88, resulting in loss of specific microRNAs.”
Why this rungThe paper directly demonstrates that F. nucleatum promotes CRC chemoresistance, that recurrent post-chemotherapy patients had higher F. nucleatum tumor abundance, and that F. nucleatum acts via TLR4/MYD88 innate immune signaling causing loss of specific miRNAs (miR-18a* and miR-4802).
Decisive span
verbatim from the cited paper
Knockdown of TLR4 or MYD88 expression blocked-induced ULK1 and ATG7 upregulation,-induced miRNA-18a* and miRNA-4802 loss, and-induced chemoresistance

1

supported-with-overreach

9 cases in corpus

Rubric: the finding is there but the claim over-asserts it: bigger effect, hardened certainty (drops a hedge), or a generalized mechanism.

AnchorA27 grader confidence: low · replay this debate →
The claim“Similarly, a previous study provided strong evidence that miR-214 could promote fibroblast differentiation of adipose-derived MSCs by restoring elastin expression to improve PFD in rats with birth trauma [31].”
Why this rungThe abstract confirms miR-214 promotes fibroblast differentiation of ADMSCs and improves PFD in birth-trauma rats, but the mechanism is stated as down-regulating Mfn2 (its target gene), not "restoring elastin expression"; elastin was one of several upregulated markers, so the claim mischaracterizes the mechanism.
Decisive span
verbatim from the cited paper
The study provided strong evidence that miR-214 could promote fibroblast differentiation of ADMSCs by down-regulating Mfn2 to improve PFD in SD rats with birth trauma.

2

partially-supported

13 cases in corpus

Rubric: the claim is multi-part and some conjuncts are supported, others not.

AnchorCh03 grader confidence: high
The claim“Studies in children showed high rate of viral/viral co-infections (18−34%) [7,8,9,19], although it should be taken into account that children included in these studies were hospitalized for respiratory infections, which could explain the high percentage of co-infections.”
Why this rungThe claim cites this paper for a viral/viral co-infection rate of 18−34%, but the paper reports a co-detection rate of 55% in its own cohort (citing 34% and 61% from other studies as comparison range). The paper does confirm the "hospitalized for respiratory infections" caveat, but its headline co-detection figure (55%) exceeds the 18−34% range attributed to it.
Decisive span
verbatim from the cited paper
Co-detections (2 or more viruses) were found in 55% (= 396) of samples.

3

technically-true-but-misleading

0 cases in corpus

Rubric: every atomic statement is literally defensible, but the framing / selection / causal language misleads.

Deliberately empty. Zero of 30 calibration cases, zero of 45 held-out cases, and zero of the C4 model’s own predictions land here. Pure “literally-true-but-misleading” framing is the hardest call to make blind, and the independent grader never escalates a case this far. citeval reports the empty rung rather than inventing one.

The closest any real case comes — A17: The claim attributes an 87.5% return-to-sport rate to Iwamoto et al with nonoperative treatment, but this abstract is a review reporting conservative-treatment return rates ranging 80.0%–89.3%; the specific 87.5% figure does not appear, and the paper is a meta-review rather than a single case series with that value. That has the flavour of this level — a claim that sounds precise and defensible — but the decider placed it one rung up at partially-supported, not the harshest literally-true-but-misleading reading.
4

unsupported-for-the-claim

13 cases in corpus

Rubric: the paper contains no finding bearing on this claim's intervention and outcome (scope held constant).

AnchorA25 grader confidence: low · the fabricated-attribution receipt →
The claim“Chen Guanghui found that Mfn2 can significantly inhibit VSMC proliferation in vitro and in vivo, and this action has nothing to do with mitochondrial fusion; Furthermore rats and humans have 95.2% Mfn2 homology 35.”
Why this rungThe abstract concerns miR-214 targeting Mfn2 in fibroblast differentiation of ADMSCs during pelvic floor dysfunction; it says nothing about Mfn2 inhibiting VSMC proliferation, its independence from mitochondrial fusion, or rat/human Mfn2 homology (95.2%). Abstract only, so confidence is low.
Decisive span
verbatim from the cited paper
The study provided strong evidence that miR-214 could promote fibroblast differentiation of ADMSCs by down-regulating Mfn2 to improve PFD in SD rats with birth trauma.

5

contradicted

2 cases in corpus

Rubric: the paper's finding is OPPOSITE in direction to the claim.

AnchorBs07 grader confidence: high · an honest loss in the tiebreak →
The claim“decreased levels of circulating triglycerides and LDL and increased levels of HDL [35].”
Why this rungThe claim asserts decreased triglycerides and LDL together with increased HDL. The cited paper does show decreased triglyceride and LDL-C in the LAB-treated group, consistent with the claim, but it explicitly reports that HDL-C was also decreased (not increased) in that group. This is a direct contradiction of a specific, material component of the claim.
Decisive span
verbatim from the cited paper
Compared with the HFD group, the HFD-LAB group had slightly decreased TC, HDL-C, LDL-C, triglyceride, glucose, leptin, AST, ALT, and lipase levels.

Two off-scale outcomes (never on the ladder)

N/A — non-substantiating
The citation is a method, dataset, tool, or background pointer — not evidence for a knowledge claim. It is not a bad grade; it is “this isn’t the kind of citation we grade.” Two independent verdicts in the held-out tiebreak are exactly this.
NOT_ENOUGH_INFO
The available text is genuinely insufficient to place a grade — an honest abstention, distinct from any grade. It weights confidence, it doesn’t fake one.

Six ordered rungs plus two honest off-ramps, holding scope constant — a resolution the field’s flat 3-way benchmarks can’t express, and the reason citeval can tell overreach from contradiction instead of lumping both under “refutes.”
