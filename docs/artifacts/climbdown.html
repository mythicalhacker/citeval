Act I · The mirage and the collapse

In-domain, the species/population scope axis scored a perfect 1.00 recall on the 30-case calibration set. That number was propped: a hardcoded shortcut, regen_results._LIVE_SCOPE_OVERRIDES, hand-pinned the answers for the cases it knew. Held out on 45 unseen cases, the same logic told the truth.

The bar that matters is the red one: on data it had never seen, real recall was 0.294, not 1.00 — a 70-point gap between the metric shown and the capability that existed.

The climb-down commit. 5ed605c — "WO-C3 scope: catch implicit-species + pregnancy/adult population over-generalizations (ext recall 0.294->0.71); de-prop cal30 scope" The override was deleted, the underlying C3 claim-scope logic was rewritten to earn the number, and the gate floor was lowered 0.95→0.90 to sit on the honest capability rather than the propped one.

The one it still misses is an honest miss. The single remaining in-domain false negative is Bp01 — a bare "general human" claim citing an infant-only paper. Flagging it would require flagging the identically-shaped controls Ce03 / Ce11 (also general-vs-pediatric), which would false-positive genuine SUPPORTED cases below the specificity floor. So it is left un-flagged, precision-first, and disclosed — not quietly patched with another override.

Act II · It happened a second time — and got the same treatment

The graded (substantive-support) axis had its own inflated tail: a handful of confident-but-wrong grades. The fix was re-measurement, not threshold-juicing. A "confident-wrong" case is defined structurally in the same gate file — self-consistency ≥ 0.85 and ordinal distance ≥ 2 from the reference.

The second climb-down commit. 8aa0ff6 — "gate: reseed cal30 baseline after C4 re-grade — confident-wrong tail cleared (qwk 0.69->0.90, adjacent 0.85->1.0, ECE 0.157->0.079); refit Platt map; update reproduction tests" The clearest case in that tail is Ce11: a split vote the pre-regrade aggregation collapsed into a silent abstention. You can replay it round-by-round — alongside the clean, unanimous A27 — and watch Ce11’s pre-regrade snapshot abstain where the fixed aggregation now grades it correctly, at an honest low tier.

Exact-match is 0.593 (16/27) and coverage 0.964 — deliberately un-juiced: the reference itself never escalates to the two harshest grades, so citeval doesn’t manufacture agreement it can’t earn. See the spin spectrum for why level 3 is empty.

Act III · Then it measured whether its confidence was even honest

Using the model’s raw self-consistency agreement directly as a confidence probability is overconfident. citeval measured the miscalibration (expected calibration error) instead of assuming it away, then fit a monotonic Platt map to correct it — and reported that AUROC is unchanged, because a monotonic map cannot change ranking.

A held-out metric collapse is old news in ML. Publishing your own — deleting the prop, lowering the gate to the honest number, and leaving the disclosure in the commit log — is the rare part, and the best reason to trust the rest of the claims.
